I. INTRODUCTION
A. Background of the Project
The Discipline Office plays an important role in maintaining student discipline, promoting proper behavior, and ensuring that school rules and regulations are followed within an educational institution. It is responsible for handling student violations, recording disciplinary incidents, monitoring student behavior, and managing the necessary documentation related to disciplinary cases. However, when these processes rely on manual record-keeping and paper-based documentation, managing student disciplinary records can become time-consuming and difficult to organize.
Manual management of disciplinary records may result in misplaced documents, duplicate entries, delayed case processing, difficulty retrieving student violation histories, and limited monitoring of unresolved disciplinary cases. Furthermore, coordinating disciplinary reports, reviewing submitted documents, scheduling meetings, and monitoring case progress may require considerable time and effort from Discipline Office personnel.
To address these concerns, the proposed project will focus on developing a centralized, web-based Discipline Office Management System using the Django framework. The system will provide a digital platform for managing student information, recording violations, monitoring disciplinary cases, and organizing related documents. It will also support authorized users in reviewing reports, tracking case progress, and maintaining organized disciplinary records. Through this system, the Discipline Office will be able to improve record management, minimize manual administrative tasks, and provide a more efficient and systematic approach to handling student disciplinary matters.
B. Statement of the Problem or Project Objectives
The main objective of this project is to design, develop, and evaluate a web-based Discipline Office Management System that will improve the management, monitoring, and documentation of student disciplinary records and violations.
Specifically, the project aims to accomplish the following objectives:
1. Develop a centralized student information management module that will allow authorized personnel to manage and retrieve student profiles and related disciplinary information.
2. Implement a violation management module that will allow authorized personnel to record, categorize, review, and monitor student violations.
3. Develop a disciplinary case monitoring feature that will enable the Discipline Office to track the status and progress of reported incidents.
4. Provide a document management feature that will organize disciplinary documents and support the submission and review of required student documents, such as apology letters, where applicable.
5. Implement user authentication and role-based access control to ensure that users will only access functions appropriate to their assigned responsibilities.
6. Develop a dashboard and reporting feature that will provide organized summaries of student violations and disciplinary records.
7. Conduct user acceptance testing with selected Discipline Office personnel and intended users to evaluate the system's usability, functionality, and performance.
II. SIGNIFICANCE OF THE PROJECT
The proposed Web-Based Discipline Office Management System will provide practical benefits to the following beneficiaries:
• Discipline Office Personnel. The system will help personnel organize student disciplinary records, manage reported violations, monitor case progress, and retrieve information more efficiently. It will also reduce repetitive manual record-keeping tasks.
• School Administrators. The system will provide organized disciplinary information that will assist administrators in monitoring student behavior, reviewing disciplinary cases, and supporting school policy implementation.
• Faculty Members and School Personnel. The system will provide an organized platform for submitting or reporting student violations, allowing authorized personnel to document incidents and coordinate with the Discipline Office.
• Students. The system will provide a more organized process for viewing relevant disciplinary information and complying with required disciplinary procedures, subject to the permissions assigned to their accounts.
• Future Researchers and Students. The project will serve as a reference for future studies and system developments involving web-based disciplinary management, student information systems, and digital record management.

IV. SCOPE AND LIMITATIONS
A. System Scope and Technical Architecture
The proposed Web-Based Discipline Office Management System will be designed to support the administrative operations of the school's Discipline Office. It will provide a centralized platform for recording, managing, and monitoring student disciplinary information.
Core Modules and Functionalities
• Student Information Management – The system will allow authorized users to manage student profiles and retrieve relevant student information.
• Violation Management – The system will provide functionality for recording student violations, identifying violation categories, attaching supporting evidence, and maintaining violation records.
• Disciplinary Case Monitoring – The system will allow authorized personnel to review disciplinary cases, update case statuses, and monitor the progress of reported incidents.
• Apology Letter Management – The system will support the submission, review, and verification of student apology letters as part of the disciplinary process.
• Meeting Management – The system will provide a feature for organizing and scheduling disciplinary meetings when necessary.
• Dashboard and Reports – The system will display relevant summaries of student disciplinary records, reported violations, and case statuses to assist authorized personnel in monitoring disciplinary activities.
• User Account and Access Management – The system will provide user authentication and role-based access to protect disciplinary information and restrict unauthorized access.
B. Technology Stack and System Architecture
The system will utilize a web-based client-server architecture. The backend will be developed using Python and the Django web framework, while the frontend will use HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Bootstrap for the user interface.
The database will use a relational database management system, with MySQL/MariaDB as the database technology indicated in the existing project setup. The system will also utilize Django's built-in authentication and administrative capabilities to manage user accounts and authorized access.

C. Target Users
The proposed system will serve the following intended users:
• System Administrator – Will manage user accounts, system configurations, and access permissions.
• Discipline Office Personnel – Will manage student disciplinary records, document reported violations, review cases, and monitor disciplinary activities.
• Faculty Members and Authorized School Personnel – Will report student violations and provide relevant incident information.
• Students – Will access permitted student-related functions, including viewing relevant disciplinary information and submitting required documents, depending on their assigned access privileges.
D. Limitations of the Project
The proposed system will focus primarily on student disciplinary record management and the administrative processes of the Discipline Office. It will have the following limitations:
1. The system will be intended for the selected educational institution and will not initially support multiple schools or campuses.
2. The system will require an internet or local network connection to access its web-based functionalities, depending on its deployment environment.
3. The system will only provide access to disciplinary information and functions based on the user's assigned role and permissions.
4. The system will not automatically determine a student's guilt or impose disciplinary sanctions. Final decisions will remain under the authority of designated school officials.
5. The system will not include integration with external student information systems, biometric devices, or third-party platforms unless these integrations are included in the approved development scope.
6. The accuracy of student information and violation records will depend on the correctness of the information entered by authorized users.
The project will focus on improving the organization, accessibility, and monitoring of disciplinary records while maintaining the confidentiality of student information.
